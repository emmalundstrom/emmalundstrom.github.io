Arsenal score more goals than they concede – but the margin has changed.

The diagram compares how many goals Arsenal has scored with how many they have conceded in the Premier League during the five most recent completed seasons. The difference between the bars is the team's goal difference: the higher the red bar and the lower the blue, the stronger the season. The best offensive season was 2023/24 with 91 scored goals, while 2025/26, on the other hand, stands out defensively – 27 goals conceded, fewer than any other team in the league that season.

Goals Scored and Conceded per Season

Unit: number of goals. Each season consists of 38 matches. Only Premier League matches are included – cup matches and European competitions are excluded.

About the Data

Time Period
The seasons 2021/22 to 2025/26, that is five completed seasons. The ongoing season 2026/27 is not included, as it is only partially played and the figures cannot be compared with the others.
Unit
Number of goals per season. No averages or percentages.
What is included
Goals scored = goals Arsenal scored. Goals conceded = goals opponents scored against Arsenal. Own goals are counted to the team that receives the goal, according to Premier League's own statistics.
Limitation
Only league matches in the Premier League (38 per season). FA Cup, League Cup and Champions League matches are not included.
Source
Premier Leagues official league tables and club statistics, premierleague.com. The numbers are checked against the season articles on English Wikipedia.
